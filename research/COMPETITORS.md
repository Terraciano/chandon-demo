# Competitor review

## Bodega Norton

- URL: https://www.norton.com.ar/turismo/
- Why comparable: large, recognizable Mendoza winery with multiple visit and gastronomy options
- Positioning: wine, landscape, history, and high-end gastronomy within an approachable tourism offer
- Structure: welcome and place, experience list, restaurant, practical booking and contact
- Trust: named spaces, concrete inclusions, route descriptions, duration, location, reservation partner
- CTA: reservation links plus tourism email for private requests
- Useful pattern: each experience explains the physical route and what is tasted
- Avoid: long undifferentiated copy blocks and repeated superlatives

## Susana Balbo Wines

- URL: https://www.susanabalbowines.com.ar/enoturismo/gastronomia
- Why comparable: Lujan de Cuyo wine tourism combining winery, food, gardens, and a relaxed outdoor offer
- Positioning: seasonal cuisine and contemporary Argentine identity, with both formal and casual formats
- Structure: restaurant story, culinary offer, outdoor experience, contact and reservation
- Trust: named restaurant, named team/family context, seasonal ingredients, direct tourism contact
- CTA: Reservas
- Useful pattern: separates the distinct moods of a destination meal and a casual picnic
- Avoid: relying on awards as the primary emotional argument

## Bodegas Salentein

- URL: https://bodegassalentein.com/es/bodega/valle.html
- Why comparable: major Mendoza wine destination where architecture, landscape, art, and hospitality shape the visit
- Positioning: Valle de Uco destination with winery, art, restaurant, and lodging context
- Structure: place-led narrative supported by destination information
- Trust: specific geography, travel distance, named venues, direct contact
- CTA: visit/contact pathways
- Useful pattern: makes the property itself part of the offer
- Avoid: legacy navigation and fragmented information across many microsurfaces

## Bodega Benegas

- URL: https://benegaswinery.com/reserva-tu-experiencia/
- Why comparable: Mendoza visit and tasting offer with history as a central reason to choose it
- Positioning: sensory journey through Argentine wine history in a historic winery
- Structure: concise experience framing followed by reservation
- Trust: real property date, family story, ambassadors, direct booking context
- CTA: Reservar experiencia
- Useful pattern: one clear narrative gives the visit meaning
- Avoid: abstract "sensory journey" language without enough practical detail

## Finca Bandini

- URL: https://fincabandini.com/visit/
- Why comparable: reservation-led Mendoza tourism with visits, tastings, lunch, and outdoor activities
- Positioning: broad, practical menu of wine and landscape experiences
- Structure: experience choices, transport/activity variants, restaurant, hours, FAQ, reservation
- Trust: published hours, reservation policy, travel time, inclusions, WhatsApp
- CTA: Reservar experiencia / Consultar por WhatsApp
- Useful pattern: practical FAQ answers the questions that block a booking
- Avoid: presenting too many variants at equal visual weight

## Category baseline

The competitive norm is not a brand manifesto. It is a decision page: show the place, name the experience, say what happens, state the duration, and provide a visible reservation path. Chandon can outperform the category by combining that clarity with the more contemporary, social visual energy already used by Chandon internationally.
