# Market research

## Scope

- Category: wine and sparkling-wine hospitality
- Subcategory: winery visits, guided tastings, food pairing, and garden experiences
- Geography: Agrelo, Lujan de Cuyo, Mendoza, Argentina
- Primary buyer: adults planning a Mendoza leisure itinerary who want a recognizable wine brand, a polished setting, and a reservation they can understand quickly
- Secondary buyer: local residents and domestic travelers planning a meal, picnic, tasting, or celebration
- Primary conversion: reserve an experience through Chandon's existing SevenRooms flow

## Evidence classification

### Confirmed public facts

- Chandon Argentina presents its Mendoza site as a "Brand Home" and offers winery and vineyard experiences. Source: https://www.chandon.com.ar/nuestra-bodega/
- The current public offer includes Visita Clasica, Visita Premium, Menu Casa Chandon, Chef de Cave, 5 Sentidos, Sweeties, Picada Argentina, and Picnic en nuestros jardines. Source: https://www.chandon.com.ar/nuestra-bodega/
- The public page describes guided vineyard and winery visits, sparkling-wine tastings, food pairings, a private cellar, gardens, and a bistro. Source: https://www.chandon.com.ar/nuestra-bodega/
- The public booking listing for Visita Clasica states a tour of the winery, gardens, oenology area, tanks and cellar, followed by a tasting of three sparkling wines. It lists the venue as RP15 km 29, Agrelo, Mendoza. Source: https://www.sevenrooms.com/experiences/chandonargentina/visita-degustacion-clasica-9359951343
- The winery page publishes a visitor email and WhatsApp contact. Source: https://www.chandon.com.ar/nuestra-bodega/
- Chandon's global brand language repeatedly centers togetherness, sparkling wine, and "a world of possibilities." Source: https://www.chandon.com/en-au and https://www.chandon.com/en-us/about-chandon-our-wineries.html

### Inference used for this sales hypothesis

- The strongest first-contact page should sell a half-day feeling before explaining every option.
- The broad supermarket presence described in the prospect brief makes approachability important. The page should feel special without implying exclusivity or scarcity.
- Visitors are likely comparing several wineries in one itinerary, so duration, inclusions, minors policy, location, and booking clarity reduce friction.

### Unknown or intentionally neutral

- Current prices beyond the linked live booking flow
- Availability by date
- Accessibility details for the Argentina property
- Languages offered for every experience
- Awards, certifications, annual visitor volume, or production figures
- Claims about founding year, oldest vines, sustainability performance, or cellar scale

## Common services

- Guided winery and vineyard tour
- Guided tasting with named bottle count
- Premium/private tasting
- Food-paired tasting
- Restaurant or set-menu reservation
- Picnic or relaxed outdoor experience
- Blending or create-your-own-wine activity
- Private and corporate events

## Expected trust signals

- Clear location and arrival information
- Transparent duration and inclusions
- Direct reservation link with live availability
- Contact channel for special experiences
- Real photography of landscape, architecture, cellar, table, and wine
- Practical policy information for minors and reservation requirements
- Named wine styles or labels only when current and verified

## Research stop condition

Terminology, buyer needs, trust signals, and the reservation-first conversion pattern repeated across Chandon, Norton, Susana Balbo, Salentein, Finca Bandini, and Benegas. Additional searching was unlikely to change the landing-page hypothesis.
