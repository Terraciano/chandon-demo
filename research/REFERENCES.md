# Selected references

## Chandon Australia

- URL: https://www.chandon.com/en-au
- Role: identity and global brand-expression reference
- Use for: bold all-caps wordmark, immersive social photography, direct "visit" language, sparkling wine shown as part of a shared table rather than a museum object
- Do not copy: campaign photography, exact headline, navigation, or product compositions
- Observed pattern: full-bleed human imagery carries the energy; typography stays simple, confident, and highly legible

## Chandon global wineries

- URL: https://www.chandon.com/en-us/about-chandon-our-wineries.html
- Role: brand and experience-language reference
- Use for: togetherness, place, immersive tastings, and the idea that each Chandon winery reflects its surrounding landscape
- Do not copy: global copy or specific estate claims
- Observed pattern: the winery is introduced through arrival, setting, food, and wine together

## Ruinart visits

- URL: https://www.ruinart.com/fr-fr/visitedescrayeresreims.html
- Role: mature sparkling-wine hospitality reference
- Use for: restrained information density, architecture as a protagonist, culture and cellar experience treated as one visit
- Do not copy: Ruinart's white palette, art program, proprietary architecture, or heritage claims
- Observed pattern: a small amount of precise text lets material, space, and photography create value

## Matias Riccitelli Wines and Bistro

- URL: https://www.argentina.travel/en/enotourism/matias-riccitelli-wines-bodega-bistro
- Role: boutique Mendoza identity reference
- Use for: contemporary local character, agroecological vineyard context, bold label sensibility, food and wine presented as an integrated visit
- Do not copy: awards, label artwork, visual marks, or sustainability claims
- Observed pattern: modernity comes from confidence and specificity, not from technology aesthetics

## Finca Decero

- URL: https://fincadecero.com/en/tourism/
- Role: place-led storytelling reference
- Use for: landscape pacing, vineyard-first narrative, concise explanation of how the visit moves from land to winery to table
- Do not copy: terroir story, vineyard name, architecture, or copy
- Observed pattern: the sequence of the actual visit can become the page's narrative structure

## Distinct jobs covered

- Brand identity and social energy: Chandon Australia
- Global brand continuity: Chandon wineries
- Sparkling-wine restraint and materiality: Ruinart
- Contemporary Mendoza character: Riccitelli
- Place-to-table story: Finca Decero
